## Notes
- Graph problems are mostly "pick the right algorithm" — the hard part is recognizing WHICH graph problem it is.
- Dijkstra vs Bellman-Ford: Dijkstra for non-negative weights (faster), Bellman-Ford when negative edges exist.
- Topological sort = the key to any "ordering with prerequisites" problem.
