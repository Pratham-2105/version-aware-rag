# Who Am I — Arjun Mehta (self-assessment, October 2026)

## The Honest Version
I'm a 3rd year CS student who's good at building things from scratch but bad at finishing them on time. I pivot too much — web dev to ML to research to AI backend. Each pivot felt right at the time and looking back they all made sense, but from outside it probably looks scattered.

## What I'm Actually Good At
- Understanding things deeply (I'd rather build from scratch than use a library I don't understand)
- Explaining technical concepts (people in my batch come to me for help)
- Writing clean code when I'm not rushing
- Competitive programming (top 15% in my batch, CF 1550)

## What I'm Bad At
- Saying no to interesting tangents (QubitML happened because it sounded cool)
- Consistency — I work in bursts, not steady hours
- Networking and self-promotion (my LinkedIn is sparse)
- Asking for help (I'll spend 6 hours debugging something I could have asked about in 10 minutes)

## What I Actually Want
A job where I build intelligent systems — not just CRUD apps, not just running sklearn. Something where understanding the math helps. AI backend, retrieval systems, maybe inference optimization someday.

An internship that proves I can do this. Income that means I'm not just a student with projects — I'm someone who gets paid for this skill.

## The Fear
That I'm too late. That the pivot cost me. That by the time I have ML projects worth showing, everyone else will already be placed. That Papa will have to keep paying for another year while I "figure it out."

## What Keeps Me Going
PixelNet. The CF 1550. Rohan believing in me. The feeling when something I built from scratch actually works.
