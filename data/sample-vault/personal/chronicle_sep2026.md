# Chronicle — September 2026

Three months into the ML pivot. No regrets.

PixelNet is done — 91.3% on CIFAR-10, every line written by me. When I explain backprop in interviews, I won't be reciting — I'll be remembering the three days I spent debugging the batch norm backward pass.

The impostor syndrome is different now. Before it was "I don't know enough web dev." Now it's "real ML people have papers and GPU clusters and I'm training on a laptop CPU." But then I look at PixelNet and think — how many people actually built a neural network from scratch? Not used PyTorch. Built it. That's real.

QubitML is... I don't know. Prof. Sharma is kind and enthusiastic but the project feels like it exists because he wanted a research student, not because the idea is strong. The results are mediocre. I don't want to hurt his feelings but I also don't want to spend months on something I can't defend in an interview.

Rohan got an internship offer. Backend Java at a Noida service company. 15k/month. He's happy. I'm happy for him. I'm also jealous and I hate that I'm jealous. My path is different. Mine takes longer. I believe that. Most days.

The CF rating drop to 1480 after hitting 1510 hurt more than it should. It's just a number. But it felt like the universe saying "you're not improving."

Started thinking about NoteFlow. I have so many files now — plans, handovers, project docs, these chronicles. I can never find anything. What if I built a tool that could actually search through all of this intelligently? Not just keyword search — something that understands what's current vs what's outdated.

Papa called again. This time he asked about "the AI thing." He doesn't understand it but he's trying. I told him it's like building the brain of a computer. He said "achha" in that way that means he trusts me even though he doesn't get it.
